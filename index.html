Why seamless matters

Most gutters are sold in short sections and pieced together on site. Every joint is a place water can work its way through, especially after a few KZN summers of sun and storms.

We form each gutter length on site, using a portable extrusion machine loaded with coil aluminium, cut to the exact size of your roof. One length, start to end. Nothing to leak.

  • Formed and cut on site to your exact measurements
  • No joints along the run means far fewer leaks over time
  • Finished in a colour to suit your roof and walls

Sectional gutters

Joins every few metres — each one a possible leak point.
